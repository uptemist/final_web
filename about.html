ABOUT WILDLIFE ENCYCLOPEDIA

Learn about animals, discover their habitats and understand why protecting wildlife matters.

OUR MISSION

Wildlife Encyclopedia is an educational project that helps people discover the diversity of animals around the world. Our goal is to make information about wildlife simple, interesting and easy to explore.

Learning about animals helps people understand ecosystems and see why protecting nature is important.

DID YOU KNOW?
